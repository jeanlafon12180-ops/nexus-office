# Licensing and third-party notices

Nexus Office is at an early stage. Its final licensing and distribution model has not yet been selected.

## Important distinction

Open-source software can be reused under the terms of its license. Those terms vary by component and by how code is modified, linked, packaged, or distributed.

LibreOffice is not governed by one blanket license for every file. Its source tree includes files under different licenses, including MPL 2.0 and LGPL-family licenses, as well as third-party components with their own notices and obligations.

## Before integrating or redistributing LibreOffice

- Identify the exact files, libraries, and runtime components used.
- Review the license of each component and any applicable exceptions.
- Preserve copyright and license notices.
- Satisfy source-code availability and notice obligations where required.
- Review trademark and branding rules separately from copyright licenses.
- Keep a third-party notices inventory for every release.

Do not assume that the entire product can be relicensed under one license without reviewing the actual dependency set.

## Current Nexus Office code

The initial shell is newly written for this repository. A repository-wide license decision is intentionally deferred until the project owner selects a license after reviewing the planned dependencies.
